## 5. How We Work

**Section label:** HOW WE WORK

**H2:** A process built to remove surprises.

**Step 01 — Strategy Call**
Thirty minutes. We ask what you sell, who buys it, and what's currently broken. No deck, no pitch.

**Step 02 — Audit & Plan**
We review your brand, channels, site and pipeline, then come back with a written plan: what we'd do, in what order, and what it costs. Yours to keep either way.

**Step 03 — Build**
Brand guidelines locked, systems set up, first creative batch produced. You approve once — then we run.

**Step 04 — Run & Report**
Content ships, campaigns run, automations handle follow-up. You get one clear monthly report: what we did, what it cost, what it returned.

**Step 05 — Scale**
We double down on what's working and cut what isn't. Every quarter we revisit the plan together.

## 8. FAQ

**Do I have to take all ten services?**
No. Most clients start with two or three and add as they grow. The advantage is that when you do add one, the team already knows your brand.

**Who pays for ad spend?**
You do, directly to the platform. We never mark it up. Our fee is for managing it.

**How fast is creative turnaround?**
Standard design and short-form video requests come back within 48 hours. Larger builds get a scheduled timeline up front.

**Do I own the accounts and assets?**
Yes, always. Ad accounts, CRM, source files, automation workflows and documentation are yours from day one.

**Is there a lock-in contract?**
Retainers run month to month after an initial three-month period — the minimum honest window to judge whether the work is landing.

**Do you work with businesses outside my industry?**
We work across industries. On the strategy call we'll tell you plainly if we're not the right fit.
