# `/about` — ABOUT

## Hero

**Eyebrow:** About us

**H1:** We built the agency we wished our clients could hire.

**Subhead:** Growthforge exists because good marketing keeps dying in the gaps between the people delivering it.

---

## Section: Origin

**H2:** Why we started

**Body:**
We spent years on the other side of this — as the designer, the editor, the ads manager, the developer. The pattern was always the same. The work itself was rarely the problem. The problem was that the person making the creative had never seen the ad account, the person running the ads had never seen the CRM, and nobody was accountable for what happened after a lead came in.

Clients weren't paying for bad work. They were paying five times over for the same context to be re-explained, and losing revenue in the handoffs nobody owned.

So we built one team that covers the whole chain — brand, creative, media, build and systems — and made a single group accountable for the outcome instead of the deliverable.

---

## Section: What we believe

**H2:** How we think about the work

**Four belief cards:**

**Systems beat campaigns.**
A great ad with no follow-up is a receipt, not a result. We'd rather build something that keeps working after the campaign ends.

**Consistency beats brilliance.**
Twelve good posts that shipped on time outperform three brilliant ones that shipped late. Reliability is a strategy.

**Honesty is cheaper than optimism.**
Telling you to kill a campaign costs us short-term revenue and earns us a long-term client. We'll take that trade every time.

**Ownership isn't negotiable.**
Your accounts, your assets, your workflows, documented. An agency that holds your business hostage isn't a partner.

---

## Section: How we're set up

**H2:** One team, not a marketplace

**Body:**
We're a small senior team, not a network of subcontractors you'll never meet. Every engagement has one account lead who knows your business and stays on it. The specialists — designer, editor, ads manager, developer, automation engineer — work from the same brand guidelines and the same monthly plan.

That's the entire operating advantage. It sounds mundane. It's the reason nothing gets lost.

---

## Section: Who we work with

**H2:** Who this is for

**Good fit:**
- Businesses spending real money on marketing who can't see what it's returning
- Founders who've outgrown doing it themselves but aren't ready for an in-house team

**Not a fit:**

We'll tell you on the first call if we don't think we're right for you.

# `/process` — HOW WE WORK

## Hero

**Eyebrow:** Our process

**H1:** A process built to remove surprises.

**Subhead:** You should never wonder what your agency is doing this month. Here's exactly how an engagement runs, from the first call to the quarterly review.

---

## Step 01 — Strategy Call
**Duration:** 30 minutes · **Cost:** Free

We ask what you sell, who buys it, what you've already tried, and what's currently broken. No slide deck, no pitch. By the end of the call you'll know whether there's a real gap we can close, and we'll say so plainly if there isn't.

**You get:** An honest read on your biggest bottleneck.

---

## Step 02 — Audit & Plan
**Duration:** 3–5 working days

We go through your brand assets, social channels, ad accounts, website and pipeline. Then we write up what we'd do, in what order, with what it costs and what we'd expect it to move.

**You get:** A written plan. It's yours to keep whether you hire us or take it to someone else.

---

## Step 03 — Build
**Duration:** Weeks 1–3 of the engagement

The setup phase. Brand guidelines locked, channels and tracking configured, CRM pipeline built, automations connected, first batch of creative produced. You review once at the end of it rather than being pinged daily.

**You get:** Brand guidelines · configured accounts · first creative batch · working pipeline.

---

## Step 04 — Run & Report
**Duration:** Ongoing, monthly

Content ships on the calendar. Campaigns run and get tested. Automations handle follow-up. At the end of each month you get one report — what we did, what it cost, what it returned, and what we're changing next month.

**You get:** A monthly content calendar · live campaigns · one plain-English report.

---

## Step 05 — Scale
**Duration:** Quarterly

We sit down every quarter and look at the whole picture. What deserves more budget, what should be cut, what service you might now be ready to add. The plan gets rewritten rather than repeated.

**You get:** A revised quarterly plan with clear priorities.

---

## Section: What we need from you

**H2:** What we'll ask of you

An engagement works when the client holds up three things. We'd rather say this up front than be disappointed later.

- **One decision-maker.** Approval by committee is what turns a two-day turnaround into a three-week one.
- **Access, early.** Ad accounts, analytics, CMS, CRM. We can't audit what we can't see.
- **Honest feedback, quickly.** "I don't like it" on day one is genuinely more useful than a polite yes and silence for a fortnight.

---

## Section: Communication

**H2:** How we stay in touch

- **Shared channel** — Slack or WhatsApp, whichever you already live in. Answered within one working day.
- **Monthly report** — one document, sent on a fixed date.
- **Strategy calls** — monthly on Spark, bi-weekly on Anvil, weekly on Furnace.
- **Escalation** — your account lead's direct line for anything urgent.
