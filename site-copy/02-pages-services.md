# `/services/brand-management`

**Breadcrumb:** Services / Brand Management
**Eyebrow:** Brand & Creative

## H1: Your brand, actively managed.

**Subhead:** Most brands get a logo and a colour palette, then drift for three years. We own your guidelines and make sure every asset that goes out actually matches them.

**CTA:** Book a Free Strategy Call

### The problem

You paid for a brand identity once. Since then, six different people have made things for you — each interpreting it slightly differently. Your Instagram doesn't look like your website, which doesn't look like your deck.

It reads as small. Customers can't tell you're inconsistent, but they can feel that something's unresolved, and it quietly costs you the premium you should be charging.

### What's included

- Brand guidelines — written, versioned and actually enforced
- Monthly content calendar built with you, not sent to you
- Campaign planning and creative direction
- Brand consistency audits across every live channel
- Messaging framework — how you describe what you do
- Single point of accountability for anything brand-facing

### What you get

A living brand guidelines document, a signed-off content calendar every month, and a quarterly consistency audit with the fixes already made.

### How it works

1. **Audit** — we collect every asset currently in the wild and mark where they diverge.
2. **Define** — guidelines get written or rewritten: logo use, colour, type, tone of voice, photography direction.
3. **Enforce** — every asset produced from here, by us or by you, is checked against the document.
4. **Review** — quarterly audit to catch drift before it spreads.

### Good fit / Not a fit

**Good fit:** You have multiple channels, more than one person making content, and a nagging sense they don't match.
**Not a fit:** You're pre-launch with no identity yet — you need a brand identity project first, which we also do as one-off project work.

### FAQ

**Do you design the logo too?**
Brand identity design is project work, quoted separately. Brand *management* is the ongoing service that keeps the identity intact once it exists.

**Can our in-house team still make things?**
Yes, and most clients' teams do. That's the point of guidelines being written down properly.

**How is this different from social media management?**
Social media management runs one channel. Brand management governs everything — including that channel.

### The problem

WordPress runs a huge share of the web for a good reason: your team can actually use it. The trouble is how most WordPress sites end up — a bought theme, twenty-eight plugins, four of them abandoned, a five-second load time and a security patch nobody's applied since 2023.

The platform isn't the problem. How it was assembled is.

### What's included

- Custom theme development, built for your content rather than bent around a template
- WooCommerce store setup and customisation
- Plugin integration, custom fields and custom post types
- Migrations from other platforms, or between hosts
- Speed optimisation — caching, image handling, database cleanup
- Security hardening, backups and ongoing maintenance

### What you get

A custom theme in your repository, a documented plugin stack with a reason for each one, a staging environment, and a maintenance schedule if you want it ongoing.

### How it works

1. **Audit** — for existing sites, we catalogue the theme, plugins, load time and vulnerabilities.
2. **Plan** — decide what's rebuilt, what's kept, and what gets deleted. Usually a lot gets deleted.
3. **Build** — developed on staging, never on your live site.
4. **Harden** — caching, backups, security and updates configured before launch.

### Good fit / Not a fit

**Good fit:** Your team publishes content regularly and needs full control without asking a developer.
**Not a fit:** You want a fast marketing site and nobody on your team will ever edit it — you'll get a better result from Website Development.

### FAQ

**Can you fix our existing site instead of rebuilding it?**
Often, yes. The audit tells us honestly whether repair or rebuild is cheaper — sometimes a rebuild costs less than repeatedly patching.

**Do you offer ongoing maintenance?**
Yes — updates, backups, uptime monitoring and security patching on a monthly plan.

**Can you make WordPress fast?**
Yes, but it means removing things. Most speed problems are plugin bloat, unoptimised images and cheap hosting.

**Subhead:** AI chat, SMS and WhatsApp agents that qualify enquiries and book them straight into your calendar. Voice agents that pick up when you can't.

Speed of response is one of the few things that reliably predicts whether an enquiry converts. And most businesses take hours — because the enquiry arrived at 9pm, or during a job, or on a Sunday.

By the time you reply, the person has messaged two competitors and booked whoever answered first. You didn't lose on price or quality. You lost on latency.

### What's included

- Website chat agents trained on your services and pricing
- SMS and WhatsApp agents
- AI voice agents that answer calls and take bookings
- Lead qualification logic built to your criteria
- Calendar booking integration
- CRM sync, so every conversation is logged against the contact
- Human handoff rules for anything the agent shouldn't handle

### What you get

A live agent on the channels you choose, a written qualification script you approved, calendar and CRM integration, and a transcript log of every conversation.

### How it works

1. **Script** — we write the qualification flow and the escalation rules, and you sign off on exactly what it can say.
2. **Build** — agent configured, connected to your calendar and CRM.
3. **Test** — we run it against real scenarios and edge cases before it faces a customer.
4. **Launch & tune** — transcripts reviewed weekly for the first month, and the script tightened.

### Good fit / Not a fit

**Good fit:** You get enquiries outside working hours, or more than you can answer quickly, and your qualification process is consistent enough to write down.
**Not a fit:** Every sale needs deep bespoke consultation from the first message — an agent would frustrate the customer, though it can still handle booking.

### FAQ

**Will customers know it's an AI?**
Yes — we build them to disclose it. Pretending otherwise backfires badly, and in some places it's a legal problem.

**What if it can't answer something?**
It hands off to a human on rules you define. It's built to escalate rather than to guess.

**Do voice agents actually sound natural?**
Modern voice models are good, with a short pause before responses. They're excellent for qualification and booking, and not a replacement for a complex sales conversation.

**What does it cost to run?**
Platform and usage costs are billed to you directly at cost. Our fee is for building and maintaining it.
