### Case study page template

**H1:** [Client] — [one-line outcome]

**At a glance:** Industry · Services used · Engagement length

**The challenge**
Two or three paragraphs on what was actually broken when they came to you. Be specific. Vague problems make the solution sound vague.

**What we did**
The work itself, in order. Include the decisions you got wrong and corrected — it reads as far more credible than a flawless narrative.

**The results**
Three or four metrics with before-and-after, plus the time period. Always state the window: "in 90 days" is a result; "increased leads by 300%" with no timeframe is noise.

**What the client said**
A real quote, with name and role. One good specific quote beats three generic ones.

## Section: Pricing FAQ

**Why aren't prices listed?**
Because the honest answer depends on channel count, creative volume and ad spend, and a number on a page would either be wrong for you or padded to be safe. You'll get a fixed monthly figure in writing after the strategy call — before you commit to anything.

**Is there a lock-in contract?**
There's an initial three-month period, then it's month to month with 30 days' notice. Three months is the minimum honest window to judge whether the work is landing — anything shorter and you're paying for setup without seeing the return.

**Can we change tiers?**
Up at any time, effective immediately. Down at the end of a billing month.

**Do you mark up ad spend?**
No. Never. You pay the platform directly on your own card.

**What if we only want one service?**
That's fine — we'll quote it standalone. Bundles exist because they're cheaper per service, not because they're compulsory.

**How does billing work?**
Invoiced monthly in advance. Project work is 50% up front, 50% on delivery.
